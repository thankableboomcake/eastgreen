Friday at East Green

Green Day

Friday is an intentional fifth school day focused on advisory, targeted academic support, enrichment, career-pathway experiences, clubs, extended assessments, student services, and flexible learning.

Advisory curriculum

Different needs at every grade.

Grade 9

Freshman Foundations

Transition, study habits, attendance, credits, involvement, goal setting, and career exploration.

Grade 10

Academic & Career Exploration

Graduation progress, career pathways, course planning, employability skills, and personal responsibility.

Grade 11

College & Career Readiness

ACT/SAT, college research, dual enrollment, scholarships, career planning, resumes, and interviews.

Grade 12

Senior Seminar

Graduation audits, applications, financial aid, scholarships, employment, budgeting, and postsecondary transition.

G2 intervention

Support is assigned when students need it.

Teachers can require Green Day intervention when essential standards are not mastered, major work is missing, an assessment must be made up, or attendance has disrupted progress.

Teacher Assigned

Students report to the designated teacher or support area for required reteaching, completion, or reassessment.

Student Choice

Students in good standing may use G2 for voluntary tutoring, independent study, project work, or counselor appointments.

Student Services

Counselors use Green Day for academic conferences, graduation planning, wellness support, and college/career work.
